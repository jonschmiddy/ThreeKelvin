- **Changed** — the autopilot is faster. From the click to settled in orbit, a flight takes 3 to 7
  seconds, down from about 14.
  Your ship still circles a world at the speed it always did. It speeds up round its orbit just
  before it leaves and slows onto the new one as it arrives. A click on a world while it slows
  onto its orbit is kept and flown once it is on it.
- **Added** — AUTOPILOT in Settings, under MOTION. CURVED, the new default, swings wide round the
  star and bends with its pull. Its wider path takes about 3 to 7 seconds from the click until
  it settles. DIRECT flies the straighter line the autopilot used to fly. Pick DIRECT to get the
  straight flights back.
- **Fixed** — while the autopilot's flight is drawn and your ship waits in orbit, a flight it
  finds that is clearly better now takes over the drawn one. It could hold on to the older flight
  when the new one only looked as good in its quick estimate.
- **Changed** — worlds go round their suns four times as fast, and so do the suns of a binary. Comets
  and your ship's own orbit keep their speed. A save from before shows its worlds in new places once.
- **Changed** — in co-op a friend's flight is drawn on the arc it flies, not cut short between
  updates, from the moment it starts round its orbit to leave until it is on the new one. A friend
  who joins while you fly sees your flight too. Everyone in a party needs this version.
