- **Added** — in co-op you can join a friend's fight after it has started. Join after the first
  turn and you arrive stunned. You have no cards, and END TURN, FLEE and HAIL are greyed until the
  enemy's turn is over. The enemy can still shoot you, and you share the loot if the fight ends
  while you wait. Your friends see STUNNED under your ship. Join on the first turn and you act at
  once.
- **Changed** — in co-op, enemies are built for the number of players in the game, not for the
  ships in the fight. Each player past the first adds 1.1 times an enemy's hull. Wrecks, event
  rewards and materials pay one more share per player. A ship joining a fight no longer makes the
  enemy bigger. A fight keeps its numbers if someone leaves or joins partway; the next fight uses
  the new count. A pilot who died, or who left for a new galaxy, is not counted. The log says how
  many crates you got.
- **Changed** — in co-op, the column of partner ships now slides in when a friend arrives. It slides
  out when they leave. Your ship and the enemies move with it, and a card you are aiming stays in
  your hand.
- **Fixed** — in co-op, a JOIN FIGHT that lands just after the last enemy dies no longer opens a
  fresh copy of that enemy. The log says "The fight ended before you got there."
- **Fixed** — after two hits landed close together, your ship could stay a fraction of a pixel off
  its place, and the enemies were laid out around the wrong spot.
