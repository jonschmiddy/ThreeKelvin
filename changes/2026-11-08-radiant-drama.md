- **Changed** — the RADIANT style is redone to be dramatic. One strong light per scene and deep
  shadow: the galaxy's core lights the arms that face it, a world has a hard line between day and
  night, and a sun's light falls off into black across the screen. The light moves: the suns flare
  every few seconds and the core about twice a minute, each lighting the gas round it, young stars
  now and then flash on the arms, and your engines light the gas behind your ship. Bright things
  glow in their own colour in stepped, dithered halos, and the shade between them goes a deep
  violet. SIMPLIFIED and PAINTED are unchanged.
