- **Added** — comets you can catch. A comet passes through a system once per visit, on the
  system's own map clock. It comes in from the edge a few seconds after the map opens, swings past
  the star with a long gold dust tail and a blue ion tail, and is gone for good about a minute later.
  Its path ahead is dotted on the sector map, and it is in LOCAL's sky where the map has it. About
  one system in ten without another science event has a reading to take at the comet. Its page
  offers three spots on the path: far for a plain reading, near for twice the reading, and at the
  star for two and a half times. Near costs hull, and at the star it costs hull and heat. Each spot
  shows when the comet gets there and when you would (IT and YOU). A spot you cannot reach first is
  greyed out as TOO LATE. Wait on your spot and the comet comes alongside in LOCAL, its coma around
  your bow and its tail across the ship, while you take the reading. Then it goes. Jon, Nov 9: "C",
  wait on its path.
