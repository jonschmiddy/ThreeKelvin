- **Changed** — on the sector map, the route line to a world you picked no longer jumps about once
  a second while your ship goes round its orbit. It holds for about three seconds at a time, and a
  second click flies exactly the line drawn. The ship goes round to where the line starts and
  leaves from there, usually within two seconds.
- **Changed** — a selected or pointed-at world on the sector map is marked with four corner
  brackets instead of a mint circle, as the star chart marks things, and the world your ship
  orbits is marked only by its orange orbit.
