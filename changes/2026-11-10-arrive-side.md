  its lit half to arrive on the lit side of its orbit, or its dark half for the dark side; hold
  Shift while you click to go round against the world's spin. The hover tag names both ("LIT
  SIDE · WITH ITS SPIN", "HOLD SHIFT: AGAINST ITS SPIN") and the flight drawn is the one
  flown. Point away from the world and the autopilot arrives as it always did. Jeremy asked;
