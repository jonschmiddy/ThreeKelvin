- **Added** — science. The top bar has a third number now, DATA, beside CREDITS and FUEL. You
  can't buy DATA or sell it for credits. You get it from readings. Look for the SCIENCE mark on a
  system's map, on a world with no air or on an asteroid belt. A reading from a distance burns
  some fuel. Going in close pays twice as much. It takes a check, and a bad one heats the ship or
  tears the hull. Each reading also has a job for someone else that pays in other ways. Survey
  film and relic fragments can be read for DATA at a Lab, too.
- **Added** — every station's Lab spends DATA. Each run is dealt a research board at launch: three
  rows, FRAME, CORE and DRIVE, with three steps in each. You buy a row's steps in order at any Lab,
  for 4, 10 and 15 DATA. They stay with you for the whole run. AUGMENT puts one more affix on a part
  you own. The Lab offers three, you pick one, and it costs 5 DATA and some credits. Each part can
  be augmented only once. STRIP takes an affix off for 2 DATA and some credits.
  When you hold 10 DATA or more, the Laboratory's line on the station's elevator reads SPEND and
  the amount, on every deck.
