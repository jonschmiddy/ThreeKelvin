- **Changed** — tooltips no longer flicker the whole time they are open. A
  tooltip opens steady, and after about 3 seconds it glitches briefly every
  3 to 4 seconds, with clean, readable text in between.
- **Added** — as your hull fails, the screen glitches more: tooltips glitch
  harder from half hull, again at 30% when the red edge comes on, and hardest
  under 15%. The rest of the screen glitches too, starting at half hull: bands
  of it tear sideways now and then and a few words scramble, more often and
  harder as the hull drops. It all settles when you repair, and reduced motion
  turns it off.
