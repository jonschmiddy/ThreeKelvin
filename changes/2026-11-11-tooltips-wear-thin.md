- **Changed** — tooltips no longer flicker the whole time they are open. A
  tooltip opens steady and only starts to glitch after it has been open about
  3 seconds, a small flick now and then.
- **Added** — tooltips glitch harder as your hull fails: more at half hull,
  more again at 30% when the red edge comes on, and under 15% they break up
  badly in short bursts, with still, readable pauses between. They settle again
  when you repair.
