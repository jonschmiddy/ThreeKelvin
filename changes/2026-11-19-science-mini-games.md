- **Added** — Science mini games on the desk: a reading's tap now plays a game on your monitor, ported from Jon's approved prototypes with their sounds. A rock site deals Coolant (turn pipes to cool the sample), Centrifuge (balance the tubes), Sample Tray (slide the canisters to get the sample out), Ice Core (stack the layers in order) or Four Kelvin (fly the ship, get the ore). Pulsar, Anomaly, Beams, Spectrum, Gravity Sling, Signal Tuning, Cutting Torch and Leak Hunt are in too, for the sky, life and wreck sites to come (`-- minigame <id>` opens any). Beams and Spectrum are laid out to fit the glass like the others. Your score and the sector decide the haul's tier; a good run plays the win, then a reward sound for the tier, and the tier pays a material. A failure or GIVE UP still pays a common one. `-- minigame coolant depth=3 sensors=1` opens one straight away; `-- sheet=MiniGameTest` is in the gate as five units. When the haul is in, GIVE UP becomes LEAVE, the one way out (Esc too); the strip is the terminal's panel colour. The desk's boot screen types the site you are reading ("> IOTA CINDER-2 I") instead of "> CULTURE".
