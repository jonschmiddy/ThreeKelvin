# StarNumbers

## Star numbers

Above `const SPREAD`:

```text
EACH SUN ITS OWN NUMBERS (Jon, Nov 23, on the chart's destination panel: "What about
mass, temp, etc.?"). STAR_FACTS gave every sun of a kind the same MASS, SURFACE and
RADIUS. Now each system's sun draws three numbers u, v, w off
Rng.derive(&"star_facts", index): positional, so the same system always reads the
same, before a visit and after, on the chart and on the sector panel, and no other
stream moves.

  yellow dwarf      mass 0.8-1.2 (u); temperature 5,300-6,000 K with the mass (u);
                    radius mass^0.8
  red hypergiant    mass 15-30 (u); radius 700-1,500 (v); 3,400-3,800 K (w)
  blue hypergiant   mass 30-60 (u); radius 25-70 (v); 20,000-30,000 K (w)
  neutron star      mass 1.3-1.6 (u); radius 10-13 km (v); 500,000-700,000 K (w)

Luminosity follows from the two, L = R^2 (T / 5,772 K)^4 in suns, so the four never
disagree. Numbers print to two significant figures, grouped by thousands, the way
RADIUS always read (1,400 SOL). A pair's star B keeps its K5 orange dwarf facts, and
the core keeps its own. The class line (G2, M4 IA...) is unchanged. OrbitPreviewTest
holds every kind inside these ranges and the numbers equal across a fresh start.
```
