THREE KELVIN
Three ways
to hold a ship
Same combat moment, three structures. Pick the one whose commitment you want to live with — the tradeoffs are real and none of them is free.
A
Two-Panel
Ship left, whatever you face right, one starfield behind both. The documented plan.
Commits to legibility. Costs you distinctiveness — it is FTL's grammar, and the panels can drift back into a spreadsheet.
B
Cockpit
No HUD. You look out through a viewport; your stats are instruments on the console around it.
Commits to the first pillar — the ship is the character — and makes heat a physical gauge. Costs flexibility: every new system needs somewhere to live on a fixed console.
C
Chart-Primary
The star chart never leaves. Encounters open as a lens over it, so you always see how deep you are.
Commits to the greed clock — depth stays visible while you decide. Costs combat its screen, and risks noise behind the fight.
