Send this back

One file, all twelve hulls — the ones you edited and the ones you left alone, so it is a complete picture rather than a diff. It writes to your downloads; tell me and I will read it from disk, no pasting. The same button is in the toolbar, which stays reachable in focus mode. The text below is a paste-able fallback.

These start where the game currently puts them. Each hull opens with its mounts seeded by running the real mounts_along() over the lines in Database.gd, so nothing is lost by switching to placing them directly — the first thing you see is the status quo.

The counts are this manufacturer's own. A slot count is WEIGHT_BASE, plus what TIER_DELTA adds at A and S, plus whatever the house itself changes — Probate trades a weapon for a utility, Redline's perk grants one. All three are read out of Database.gd when this page is built, so they are right for whoever it was built for. The panel marks a slot red when it is short.

A thruster placed left of the hull will be clipped. The hulls are authored bare, with no canvas around them, and ShipView._paste() drops anything at a negative offset. The bench shows the overhang and measures it, because that clearance is a real thing §4 specifies and the sprites do not yet carry.
