Three Walkers

Every cycle that survived, walking your vending line at 1× — the size the room draws. Each moves at its own measured stride with the body stepped, which is the only setting where the planted boot does not creep. The numbers are underneath; the answer is on the screen.

Zoom to inspect, half speed to watch a single step.

what to look for

The feet — does a boot hold the deck while it is down, or does it shuffle? That is the fault none of the post-processing could remove.

The face and collar — do they stay the same man frame to frame, or simmer? That is the generator redrawing him each frame; 56% is the best any of these manage where a posed sprite holds about 95%.

And whether any of it matters at this size. Every measurement I have is of a sprite strip. None of them is of a figure behind glass in a dim room, which is the only condition that counts.
