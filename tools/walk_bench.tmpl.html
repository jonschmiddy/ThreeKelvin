Walk Lines

People are drawn as blocks — exactly as tall as the line says and nothing else, so the eye compares them against the doorways and railings behind them instead of reading a figure. The colour beside each line says which rung that height snaps to (57, 80, or 57 doubled to 114) and how far it has to move: green under 5%, amber under 12%, red beyond. A red line needs its own drawing; nudge it toward a rung and it comes free.

Click the picture where a floor is. That puts a line there and stands people on it. Drag the line to move it, drag the handle at the top of the tick to say how tall a person is standing there — that height is also the depth, so a shorter line's people automatically walk slower and wash out into the air. Arrow keys nudge the selected line, Delete removes it. Work is kept in this browser; save puts walk-lines.json in your downloads.
