The nebula gate did not exist until tonight

Every node already carries in_nebula. It is generated, saved, drawn on the chart, and printed in the destination panel's WARNING row — and no encounter could ask about it, because admits() had no clause for it. So the category had zero content and no way to write any.

Added, with the schema taught to accept it. That is why nebula is the one bar starting from nothing.

The eighty

Thirty for the sky, thirty for depth, twenty for tags. A slot satisfies one line only — a red-star fight is not counted twice.

Sky — red, blue, giant, pulsar, nebula, fauna302 ROUGH → 2 LETHAL each, by what the sky suits

Tags — fight and hazard2012 fight across all five · 8 hazard at EASY and ROUGH

Depth is weighted to LETHAL and BRUTAL because that is where the free pool is thinnest, and to EASY because sixteen is the smallest pool in the game and it is the one every player meets first.

What "written properly" means

Six rules, every one of them learned by getting it wrong on the four encounters already done to standard.

1
Detail is not compression. A clause earns its place by parsing on the first read. Four lines had to be rewritten for this — squeezing a plain sentence until it sounds clever costs a second pass, and the second pass is where a player stops reading encounters at all.
2
No dominated choice. Twenty-two of the current forty-seven have an option that costs nothing and gives nothing beside one that gives something. Do not add more.
3
Three choices, three kinds of question. A check you might fail is not the same question as a resource you must spend, which is not the same as a thing you decline.
4
Credits come from people. Deep space pays in cargo. Seven encounters currently hand you money with nobody in the body to hand it over.
5
Never reach into the hold. The hold is the player's. Nothing takes an item the player did not choose.
6
The player never leaves the ship. No hands, no boarding on foot, no corridors. A grapple, a cutter, a dish and a board. And a ship is it — people get the pronouns.

Checking it. python tools/encounter_lint.py --strict finds every fault a machine can name — dominated choices, untagged walk-aways, credits paid to an empty sky, out-of-band checks, hold grabs. It found 35 rulings and 15 review items on the existing 47 in one run. Run it before anybody reads a word.
