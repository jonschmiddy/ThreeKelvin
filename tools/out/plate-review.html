Rooms and Backdrops

Forty takes, one per idea. A room is the shop's own wall and floor, generated together so they match, already cut to the panel's 740×431 with the join on the floor line. A backdrop is the place on the other side of the glass — you only ever see it through the openings, so the lightbox has an in the wall view that shows just that. Keep what you want, cut the rest; the picks are saved as you go.
