Fire Solution was fired twice this round, and I did not show you the first four

They came back with a big white dial face. I had asked for a face that was “clean and uncluttered”, which is one more way of describing an absence — the same mistake as “thinning away to nothing” on Unmade, now written three separate ways. Refired with dark smoked glass: 0% pale on all four.

Worth knowing that my white check only caught one of those four. The dial is enclosed by its bezel, so the white never touches the frame border — and border-connectedness is exactly what that check tests. It is blind to a white hole in the middle of a picture, and that is a limit of the idea rather than a threshold to tune: a legitimate card can be mostly pale (frost, a lit screen) and a broken one can be pale nowhere near an edge.
