Round 3 - Hairline replaced, seven malfunctions

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

The text on Hairline was something I asked for. Its prompt says “Rivets and stencilled lettering around it” — and the brief boilerplate said it too, in scale (“bolt heads, weld seams and stencilled lettering give the size”) and in world (“Weathered plating with stencils and decals”). Every malfunction brief carried both. 191 takes were generated asking for text.

But the word alone is not the whole story, and the counter-examples are on your own cards: Dross reads SECTOR-4 / WASTE and Deadcell reads UNIT 4, both clean, both from prompts that asked for lettering. The difference is room. Hairline had a wide flat empty panel across the middle of the frame, and the model filled it with three long lines. So this round changes two things: no lettering is requested, and every prompt says the frame is crowded to every edge with no blank surface — nowhere for text to go even if it wants to write some.

Style reference is now Slag rather than full_auto — the family’s own best card, and the one you said looks perfect. Ghost is the exception: it is a display face, not machinery, so it took the Range Card reference instead.

Measured across all 36: no letterboxing, no edge bars, no white above 4%. That check is clean for the first time this project, which is what losing the lettering request bought.

Hairline has eight takes (two prompts × four) because it is a replacement rather than a first attempt; the other seven have four each.
