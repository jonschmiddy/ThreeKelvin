Reroute - fewer, bigger jacks

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

The squashed circles are arithmetic, not prompting

Measured on the take you have: the jack field sits on a six-pixel pitch, so each ring gets three or four pixels of its own. A four-pixel circle is a square with the corners knocked off; a three-pixel circle is a plus sign. There is no wording that makes one round — and the eight takes off the identical prompt I fired before this changed nothing, which is the evidence rather than the theory.

The only fix is fewer, larger jacks so each has the pixels to be round. 1–4 cut the field to eight big sockets. 5–8 cut it to two filling the frame. Both give every ring 8–12 px, and every circle in this round is properly round.

The cost is the reading, and that is the real choice

The card is Draw 1 off a Signal Board — “sorts what the sensors are shouting about into an order”. The picture you have says one cord moved among many at a glance, which is exactly the card. Eight jacks keeps most of that. Two jacks is a handsome pair of sockets that says much less about sorting — the circles are the roundest on the page and the meaning is the thinnest.

So this is not a pick-the-prettiest; it is whether round circles are worth the reading. The card as it stands is in the row on the left for comparison.
