Ship's kit round 2 - the six that went zero

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

Seven landed and are installed — Bleed Heat, Blow Out, Bulwark, Emergency Vent, Field Weld, Fire Director, Hold Fast. That is 55 of 76. This round is the six families that went zero, each rewritten from what its own tiles were actually showing.

“Dig in looks like dirt” — and two words in my prompt put it there

The card is called Dig In, and I then asked for “a lip of torn metal” with “amber rust in the torn metal”. Between them those describe broken earth almost exactly, and three of the four came back as brown rubble with roots in it. Rewritten with no digging and no rust anywhere: broad flat pads clamped on a bare steel deck, the plate dished under them, clean grey steel throughout.

The other five, read off the tiles

Bore Sight and Range both came back as generic boxy instruments. “A servo stopped on its mark” and “a drum in its detent” name mechanisms, not pictures — so the model drew a housing, which is the only part of a mechanism it can see. Both now name the readable thing: an index mark sitting on a scribed line; a pointer resting on one tick of a graduated ring. (Tick marks are shapes, not glyphs, so they are safe where numbers are not.)

Reroute drew the board four times and left the cable invisible. The lead is now lifted in a high arc with its plug in mid-air. Shunt drew one diagonal pipe with a yellow stripe, four times; it now says two bars converging from opposite corners into one clamp. Patch buried the clamp behind a white plume; the jaws now pinch the hose visibly flat, and the plume is excluded.

No white flags in this round or the last — 80 takes clean in a row.
