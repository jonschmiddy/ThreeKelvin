Walk Lines

People are drawn as blocks — exactly as tall as the line says and nothing else, so the eye compares them against the doorways and railings behind them instead of reading a figure. The colour beside each line says which rung that height snaps to (80 as drawn, or 160 doubled) and how far it has to move: green under 5%, amber under 12%, red beyond. A red line needs its own drawing; nudge it toward a rung and it comes free.

Click the picture where a floor is. That puts a line there and stands people on it. Drag the line to move it, drag the handle at the top of the tick to say how tall a person is standing there — that height is also the depth, so a shorter line's people automatically walk slower and wash out into the air. Arrow keys nudge the selected line, Delete removes it. Every change is saved as you go, straight to this page, where Claude reads it to install; save and copy still hand you a file if you want one.
