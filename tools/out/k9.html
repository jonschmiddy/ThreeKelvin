Range triangulated, Foreknowledge watching

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

Range: subject 07, and I think it worked

Two sensor pylons on the outside plating with cables converging on one pinned bracket. Takes 1–4 are the wide view with a pylon at each side; 5–8 come in close on the point where the cables meet.

Laying the 74 drawn cards side by side is what actually broke this open, and it is worth saying plainly: the instrument family was full. Range Card is already the amber plot screen, Cold Read the scope view, Bore Sight the sight ring, Cold Sights the recessed optic, Fire Solution the dial, Ghost the CRT, Reroute the patchbay. Every optical framing I reached for across 64 takes was one you had already approved on a different card. It was never going to feel like a new card. Nothing about the prompting was going to fix that.

Foreknowledge: closer, and I do not think it is there

Takes 5–8 are the subject as written — many unlit lenses in a dark recess. At 92×60 they came back as a grille or a socket panel, because an unlit lens at this size is just a hole.

Takes 1–4 add the one thing that makes an eye read as an eye: a single tiny catchlight on each curve. That is a real improvement and 1 and 4 do now read as something glinting back at you. But I have told you three rounds running that a Foreknowledge batch was improving and been wrong each time, so: my read is that this is still a panel of dots, not a thing watching. Judge it yourself and overrule me if you disagree.

If you agree it is not there, the two options I would go to next are both from the same list and neither needs a lens: 04, what it is wired to — the shard out of frame, every cable leaving its recess brand new against filthy machinery — and 10, the room it changed, the deck worn smooth into a path leading to one alcove and nothing else touched.
