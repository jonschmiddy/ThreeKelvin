Round 5 - Ghost and Hard Line rethought, four more brace

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

Five landed again — 16% of 32. Hairline, Bulkhead, Sink Plate, Standfast and Torn Liner are installed, which puts the project at 39 of 76. The brace finding held: all four brace prompts off the dense Slag reference filled their frames, none letterboxed, and three of the four landed first time.

Ghost Signal is 0 for 8, and the palette is why

I asked for “sickly green phosphor”. There is no green in this game. card_batch --palette lists 29 colours and every cool one of them is a blue-grey — and green had two separate ways to die anyway, since style_copy includes color_palette and --post snaps to that same set. It was never going to arrive.

What arrived instead: a black circle with white dots in it, four times, which reads as a porthole onto stars rather than an instrument. So this attempt drops colour as a cue entirely, makes the screen rectangular, and adds scan lines and a grid to say instrument by shape alone. The doubled contact with its smear is the subject.

Hard Line was drawn as the wrong card

“Current bleeding blue along its length” hit the same wall — no bright blue in the palette either, so it snapped to near-white and came back as arc-flash. Which is a picture the game already has: it looks like Arc Fault. And Hard Line is a brace card (Brace 5. Block 4.), so it should read as mass and boltedness, not as an electrical event. That was my brief being off-message, not the generator missing. Rewritten as structure, with arcing explicitly excluded.

One thing to watch, not yet a finding: across rounds 3 and 4, five of your ten keeps were candidate #4 of four, against 2.5 expected. At n=10 that is suggestive and nothing more — I am not filtering on it, just counting.
