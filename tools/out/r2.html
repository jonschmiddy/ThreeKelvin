Four more, and two of them change what the card is about

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

Auspice, Cold Read and Cutting Beam are installed. Walking Fire and Slug keep the art they already had, as you said. These four are what is left.

The two Singing Core cards move the subject off our own ship

“A LOT of damage being done to a ship with a burst of energy” and “vibrated to destruction, or torn in half”. Both of those put the subject on the target, which no other card in the set does — all 76 are our own machinery seen close. That is exactly why these two never worked as abstract light: they were never about our hardware.

Sympathetic Burst is three bursts blowing through one ship at once, the body buckling between them — three, because the card is Deal 7 × 3. Resonance is a ship cracked through the middle with the halves twisting apart and plating peeling off in sheets, and no fire anywhere, because being shaken apart is not the same picture as being blown up.

Style reference is Ordnance — the only accepted card with a vehicle in the void at modern density. The five banked ship pictures are all from the sparse era and would have dragged the old look straight back in.

Drumfire loses the gun entirely

Last round it came back as four side-on gun portraits, because I wrote “autocannon”. It is now the mechanism from above — drum turning, belt climbing out, casings pouring down the chute — with no muzzle in the frame at all, off the Slag reference.

Every prompt this round went through card_batch.py --check before firing. All four clean.

Cold Store went 0 for 4 with no note, so I changed the subject rather than the wording: a rack of cryogenic cells furred with rime, one cracked and venting sideways, instead of a lone block with a frost ring.
