what to look for

Is it a person, or a smear? At this size a body has about twenty pixels of width to say who it is. The named garments are what carry it — an apron, a tabard, a coil of strap.

Does it read as different from the one beside it? Eight silhouettes that all say "adult in a coat" is one character generated eight times.

Height is not a fault here. These run 75–85px against an 80px line, and that spread is what a crowd of people looks like. Only a figure well outside it is wrong.
