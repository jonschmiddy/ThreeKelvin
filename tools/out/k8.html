Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

74 of 76. Bore Sight and Fire Solution are in. These two are the last.

Foreknowledge stops being a cube

Six takes of “a faceted block” all came back as a plain grey box — and at 2× a plain grey box reads as a shipping crate, which is the least interesting alien geometry available. The word faceted produced it every single time.

Now an irregular blade-like shard with nothing box-like about it, plus one thing borrowed from direction A that B was missing: a fine bright line projecting out of it onto a plate and ending in a marked spot. The module's flavour is “it tells you where the shot goes; it does not say how it knows” — the picture now shows the telling.

Range: I was judging at the wrong size

The split field has been right since round 6. The problem was that the two craft were too small to read at 2× — a big empty circle with two specks in it. I had been looking at these tiles at 4×, where they were fine. The repo has a rule about exactly this and I was not following it.

Making them large turned them into solid black blobs with no internal shape, which was worse. Size alone was never the answer: it is pale outline with a lit spine and two engine points, about a third of the field wide. Readable at 2×, and unmistakably two copies of one thing being slid together.

If both land, the set closes at 76 of 76. Only your Sort pick stays open.
