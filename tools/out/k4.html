Ship's kit round 4 - thirteen cards, one per note

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

Five installed — 62 of 76. Absolute, Patch, Scuttle, Spent Brass and Truss. Thirteen cards re-fired here, one per note.

The white on Cold Sights and Range was a letterbox, and my prompt caused it

Both said the subject was “seen from the front” / “seen close from the side”. That frames a thing as an isolated asset, and card_batch already records that pro letterboxes an object on white when the prompt describes a thing with nothing around it. The crowding clause I bolt on the end could not outweigh the subject clause — Cold Sights came back 33–50% white with the lens sitting in the middle like a sprite on a sheet.

Fixed by embedding instead of viewing: the optic is recessed into armour that fills the picture; the rangefinder is built into a mounting block that fills the picture. No viewing angle named at all. All 52 takes this round are clean.

The object notes

Bore Sight was an ACOG because “optical sight on a machined rail with turrets” is a rifle scope — it is now the size of a bulkhead door and set into ship structure. Chain Feed drew a bicycle chain; it is an ammunition belt now, cartridges seated nose-forward in steel links. Feed lifted a barrel; it lifts a single brass cartridge off a rack of them. Fire Solution had junk on the plotting plate; the plate is bare but for its engraved arcs. Overside's rails were impossible; two rails, exactly parallel, exactly level, no bends. Foreknowledge caged the shard like a trophy — the block is now built into the ship, plating flush against its faces and conduits running inside it.

The colour notes

Deep Song and Whale Song both rendered the organic as white plastic and amber tubing. You said you love the machine-and-flesh mix and the yellow is wrong, so both now say dull grey-pink, matte, wet, and explicitly never white, never amber, never yellow. Reroute's socket light was a big bloom; it is one small flat pip with no halo now.

Two you did not comment on both went 0-for-4, so I read their tiles: Overwatch was flat floor-tiling with no depth — now a curved bank at a raking angle with void along the top edge. Set Feet was static and symmetrical — the springs are crushed with the top plate driven out of level.
