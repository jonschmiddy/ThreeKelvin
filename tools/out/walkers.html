Dock Hand Rungs

Each cycle on the vending deck at 1×, the size the room draws, walking at 20 px/s and standing on the walk line nearest its own height. Every one moves at its own measured stride with the body stepped — the only setting where the planted boot does not creep. The numbers are underneath; the answer is on the screen.

what to look for

The feet. Does a boot hold the deck while it is down, or shuffle under him? That is the fault no post-processing has ever removed — and boot misses by is the number that predicts it: how far the planted boot travels against how far it should. Under 25% reads clean, above it reads as a skate. Calibrated on the four you called by eye.

The pace of the steps. Under about 5 a second the eye reads footsteps; over it, a shimmer. That number, not the count of faulty frames, is what decided the last round.

Whether he is the right size for the line. The percentage says how far over the rung each one stands — pixel art cannot be scaled by anything but whole numbers, so this is the only chance to fix it.
