Unmade - the last approved brief

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

The last of the thirty-one briefs you approved. Unmade is the only one left; the sixteen malfunctions and the rest of brace are drawn and pushed (d4f35d0), 47 of 76.

The brief asked for violet and I did not. It says “a violet volume in which debris and a shockwave are dissolving to nothing”, and there is no violet in the palette any more than there was green — two brace prompts ending “the violet the only colour” came back 75% white across all eight candidates, which is on record in card_batch. So the field is described as an effect instead of a colour: a hard invisible boundary, debris breaking into coarse grain and thinning away as it crosses, everything clean on the far side.

Three of the four flag for white joined to the border — 2, 3 and 4. That is the new connectedness check doing its job, and worth knowing before you look: dissolving debris against a boundary is exactly the subject most likely to produce a real background, so the flag may be describing the picture rather than a fault. Judge it on the tile.
