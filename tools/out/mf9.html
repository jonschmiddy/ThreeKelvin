Unmade - the far side named

Every take sits beside the card as the game draws it today — the orange tile heading each row, at the same zoom as the candidates, so the comparison is against the thing being replaced rather than against the other options.

You were right and I was wrong to hedge. My check flagged three of the four last time and I wrote in the notes that the flag “may be describing the picture rather than a fault”. It was not. A card is full bleed and opaque; white is only ever undrawn canvas.

“Nothing” is a word that produces white

The prompt said the debris was “thinning away to nothing”, and the model drew nothing as blank canvas — the only absence it has.

This is the letterbox rule one level down. card_batch already said a prompt must name what fills the frame around the subject; what it did not say is that anywhere the picture says a thing stops, ends, dissolves or runs out, the prompt has to say what is there afterwards. Both prompts here name the far side positively: deep indigo void, cold stars, a rusty amber nebula wash, never blank and never white.

Seven of the eight now have a starfield where the white was. Take 8 still flags at 36% and is the exception, not the rule.

Takes 1–4 are the first prompt — wreckage granulating into stars across a boundary. Takes 5–8 are the second, a single slab of plating with one frayed edge against the void, which reads quieter and less like the card's “Brace 10. Block 10.”
