SCIENCE DESK · POWER-ON

Picking science on LOCAL zooms into the ship and fades to the desk, and the desk powers on.

1 · THE FOUR ROOMS, UNLIT

The finished art with no lighting, the game screen composited into the glass.

2 · C LIGHTING, ALL FOUR ROOMS

Jon's pick C in every room: hand-shaded ramps, cool and violet in shadow, warm where lit. Each light falls off by distance and angle from its own source, with stepped, dithered bands; the lamps and string lights flicker a little; dust in the air; living portholes. The keyboards are drawn in code. Move onto a room to play it, click it to see it big.

3 · KEYBOARDS AND THE SQUARE LIGHT, BEFORE / AFTER

Each keyboard is now drawn in code: keycaps with a lit top and a darker front lip, a case with a front face and a shadow on the desk. The square light edges were box and row cuts in the lighting code; they are gone, and every light now falls off by distance and angle. 2x crops.

4 · NORMAL MAPS

PixelLab normal maps, 1:1 with the art (red is right, green is up, blue faces you).
